TUBITAK Signing Helper — Apple Silicon

Drag TUBITAK Signing Helper.app to Applications, then open it there.

Java is included. On first use, the helper downloads and verifies the original
signing client directly from TÜBİTAK.

For AKİS cards, use the app's Install AKİS driver button if the ARM driver is
missing. Complete the official installer and macOS administrator approval.

Download a fresh .jnlp from TÜBİTAK, open it with the helper, review the documents,
then enter your USB card PIN in TÜBİTAK's signing window. Check the result on the
TÜBİTAK website.

This is an unofficial community helper, independent of TÜBİTAK and Kamu SM.
Source, notices and support:
